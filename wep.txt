2) Convert DPS + speed → damage

Given weapon speed S:

Average hit: AvgHit = DPS * S

Tooltip range (Min/Max) is not uniquely determined by DPS+S alone; items author a damage range and DPS is derived from it.

Note: caster weapons that “sacrifice DPS”

Some caster weapons are described as sacrificing melee DPS down to ~41–42 DPS to buy spell stats; if you model caster weapons, you may need that adjustment
