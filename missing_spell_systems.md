# Missing Spell Systems

Grouped by the underlying system required, not by class. Spells listed under each are representative examples, not exhaustive.

Movement speed slows and rage are **already implemented** and are excluded. Ground-target AoE, DoTs, HoTs, and projectiles are also already covered.

---

## 1. Combo Points
A secondary resource generated by certain attacks and consumed by finishing moves.

**Generating:** Sinister Strike, Backstab, Ambush, Garrote, Rake, Hemorrhage, Claw, Shred, Pounce  
**Consuming (finishing moves):** Eviscerate, Rupture, Kidney Shot, Slice and Dice, Expose Armor, Ferocious Bite, Rip

---

## 2. Stealth System
An entity state that hides the player from enemies beyond a detection range. Breaks on damage or certain actions.

**Entry:** Stealth, Prowl, Vanish  
**Requires stealth (`CastCondition.WhileStealthed`):** Ambush, Cheap Shot, Garrote, Sap, Pounce, Ravage, Pickpocket, Premeditation  
**Reacts to stealth breaking:** Distract

---

## 3. Stances and Shapeshift Forms
A state machine that changes which abilities are available, may modify stats, and for shapeshifts changes the entity's form entirely.

**Warrior stances:** Battle Stance, Defensive Stance, Berserker Stance  
**Druid forms:** Bear Form, Cat Form, Moonkin Form, Travel Form, Aquatic Form  
**Other:** Shadowform (Priest), Ghost Wolf (Shaman)

---

## 4. Crowd Control States
Entity states that suppress movement and/or actions. Sub-types: stun, incapacitate, fear/flee, sleep, polymorph, banish. Most break on damage; banish and polymorph do not.

`HasControl` in EntityMovement.cs is already a placeholder for this.

**Stuns:** Cheap Shot, Kidney Shot, Charge, Intercept, Hammer of Justice, Concussion Blow, Pounce  
**Incapacitates (break on damage):** Blind, Sap, Gouge, Wyvern Sting (sleep phase), Repentance, Hibernate, Shackle Undead  
**Fears:** Fear, Psychic Scream, Howl of Terror, Intimidating Shout  
**Polymorph-type (heal while CC'd):** Polymorph  
**Banish (damage-immune stasis):** Banish  
**Mind control:** Mind Control

---

## 5. Interrupt and Silence
Stopping an active channel/cast and optionally locking a spell school for a duration.

**Interrupts:** Kick, Counterspell, Earth Shock, Shield Bash, Pummel  
**Silences:** Silence (Priest), Garrote (silence on opener)

---

## 6. Damage Absorption (Absorb Shields)
A layer before health that absorbs incoming damage, potentially from specific schools only.

**All-school:** Power Word: Shield  
**Mana-fed:** Mana Shield  
**School-specific:** Fire Ward, Frost Ward, Shadow Ward

---

## 7. Buff Dispel Classification
Buffs and debuffs need a tag (Magic, Poison, Curse, Disease, Bleed) so targeted removal spells know what to remove.

**Remove from allies:** Dispel Magic, Cleanse, Purify, Abolish Poison, Abolish Disease, Cure Disease, Cure Poison, Remove Curse  
**Strip from enemies:** Purge, Tranquilizing Shot (removes enrage)  
**Consumes specific debuff type:** Conflagrate (requires Immolate on target)

---

## 8. Stat-Modifying Buffs and Debuffs
Buffs that modify primary/secondary stats on the owner: attack power, spell power, armor, crit, resistances, attack speed, etc. Movement speed is already handled; nothing else is.

**Buffs (increase stats):** Battle Shout (AP), Mark of the Wild (all stats), Blessing of Might (AP), Blessing of Kings (all stats), Arcane Intellect (Int), Power Word: Fortitude (Stamina), Inner Fire (armor/SP), Slice and Dice (attack speed), Tiger's Fury (Cat AP), Aspect of the Hawk (ranged AP)  
**Debuffs (reduce stats):** Sunder Armor (stacking armor reduction), Expose Armor, Demoralizing Shout/Roar (AP reduction), Thunder Clap (attack speed reduction), Faerie Fire (armor, no-stealth), Curse of Weakness (AP reduction), Scorpid Sting (hit reduction), Hunter's Mark (ranged AP taken increase)  
**Healing reduction debuffs:** Mortal Strike, Wound Poison

---

## 9. Additional CastConditions
New enum values with matching event subscriptions in `RegisterReactiveHook`.

| Condition | Spells |
|---|---|
| `AfterEnemyDodge` | Overpower (Warrior) |
| `AfterBlockDodgeOrParry` | Revenge (Warrior) |
| `AfterEnemyMiss` | Mongoose Bite (Hunter) |
| `WhileStealthed` | See §2 above |
| `TargetBelowHealthThreshold` | Execute (Warrior, <20%), Hammer of Wrath (Paladin, <20%) |

---

## 10. Shared Cooldown Groups
Multiple spells sharing a single cooldown timer, independent of individual spell cooldowns.

**Shaman shocks:** Earth Shock, Frost Shock, Flame Shock (all share a short CD)  
**Paladin seals:** Seal of Righteousness, Seal of Command, etc. (Judgement resets to allow a new seal)  
**Warrior stances:** switching has a shared GCD-like lockout

---

## 11. On-Hit / Swing Proc Effects
A buff that registers an `AttackHitEvent` subscription and fires an effect with a chance (or always) on each melee swing.

**Proc damage/healing:** Seal of Command, Retribution Aura, Lightning Shield, Thorns, Touch of Weakness  
**Proc extra attacks:** Windfury Weapon, Windfury Totem  
**Proc resource:** Seal of Light (heals), Seal of Wisdom (restores mana)

---

## 12. Resource Conversion Effects
A new `SpellEffect` subtype that transfers between health, mana, and other resources.

**HP → Mana:** Life Tap  
**Pet Mana → Self Mana:** Dark Pact  
**Mana → HP (large self-heal):** Lay on Hands  
**Drain enemy Mana, gain Mana:** Drain Mana, Mana Burn  
**Drain enemy HP, gain HP:** Drain Life  
**Rage → HP:** Frenzied Regeneration

---

## 13. True Mid-Channel Ticking
Effects that fire at intervals *during* a channel rather than only on completion. The current system triggers the spell on channel end.

**Channeled damage:** Mind Flay, Arcane Missiles, Blizzard, Volley, Hurricane, Hellfire, Magma Totem pulse  
**Channeled healing:** Tranquility, Drain Life (heal portion)  
**Channeled resource drain:** Drain Mana, Drain Life (damage)

---

## 14. Resurrection Mechanics
A dead entity state and the ability to revive it (with optional combat restriction for Rebirth).

**Target-rez:** Resurrection (Priest), Redemption (Paladin), Ancestral Spirit (Shaman), Rebirth (Druid, in-combat)  
**Self-rez:** Reincarnation (Shaman), Soulstone Resurrection (Warlock)

---

## 15. Pet / Companion System
Summon a persistent companion entity that persists between casts, can be commanded, and has its own stats and abilities.

**Hunter:** all pets (Call Pet, Dismiss Pet, Revive Pet, Mend Pet, Beast Training)  
**Warlock:** Summon Imp, Voidwalker, Succubus, Felhunter; Health Funnel, Dark Pact

---

## 16. Totem System
Place a special static object at your position with a limited duration that pulses or provides an aura.

**Shaman:** all totems (Searing Totem, Earthbind Totem, Windfury Totem, Healing Stream Totem, etc.)

---

## 17. Threat Values
Currently aggro tracks *who* is on the table but not *how much* threat each has. True threat numbers are needed for threat-reduction/increase spells.

**Reduce threat:** Feint, Fade, Cower, Blessing of Salvation, Tranquil Air Totem  
**Increase threat:** Righteous Fury, Sunder Armor (high threat), Distracting Shot  
**Taunt (set threat to top):** Taunt, Growl, Challenging Shout/Roar, Mocking Blow, Stoneclaw Totem

---

## 18. Bleed Damage Type
Bleeds are physical DoTs that bypass armor and interact with bleed-specific mechanics (Rip consume, bleed dispel).

**Bleeds:** Rend, Rupture, Rip, Rake, Garrote (bleed portion), Pounce (bleed portion), Hamstring (damage component counts as bleed in some contexts)

---

## Notes
- **Aura spells** (Battle Shout, Mark of the Wild, Devotion Aura, etc.) technically just need §8 stat-modifying buffs — the "apply to party" part is a targeting mode (`AcceptableTargets` + a radius-based multi-cast at the cast site), not a new system.
- **Mounts** (Summon Warhorse, Summon Felsteed, etc.) are a movement speed multiplier plus a special entity state — trivial once the movement system is mature.
- **Teleports/Portals** are map-transition infrastructure, independent of the spell system.
- **Item-creating spells** (Conjure Food, Create Healthstone, etc.) need an inventory-write effect type, which is minor once the item system is solid.
