# WoW Classic Vanilla Talents Reference

This file lists the **Vanilla / Classic Era (1.12-style)** talent trees for all 9 original classes.

Descriptions are **normalized one-line effect summaries** rather than full rank-by-rank tooltip text. They are intended as a readable reference file.

---

## Druid

### Balance
- **Improved Wrath** — Reduces the cast time of Wrath.
- **Nature's Grasp** — Gives attackers a chance to be rooted when they hit you.
- **Improved Nature's Grasp** — Increases the chance for Nature's Grasp to trigger.
- **Improved Entangling Roots** — Gives Entangling Roots a chance to avoid interruption from damage while casting.
- **Improved Moonfire** — Increases Moonfire damage and critical strike chance.
- **Natural Weapons** — Increases physical damage dealt in all forms.
- **Natural Shapeshifter** — Reduces the mana cost of shapeshifting.
- **Improved Thorns** — Increases damage caused by Thorns.
- **Omen of Clarity** — Grants a chance to enter a Clearcasting state, making your next ability or spell free.
- **Nature's Reach** — Increases the range of Balance spells.
- **Vengeance** — Increases the critical strike damage bonus of Starfire, Moonfire, and Wrath.
- **Celestial Focus** — Reduces Starfire cast time and gives it a chance to stun.
- **Nature's Grace** — Spell criticals reduce the cast time of your next spell.
- **Moonglow** — Reduces the mana cost of your damage and healing spells.
- **Moonfury** — Increases damage done by Starfire, Moonfire, and Wrath.
- **Moonkin Form** — Shapeshift into Moonkin Form, increasing armor and granting a spell-crit aura to allies.

### Feral Combat
- **Ferocity** — Reduces the Rage or Energy cost of Maul, Swipe, Claw, and Rake.
- **Feral Aggression** — Increases Ferocious Bite damage and the effect of Demoralizing Roar.
- **Feral Instinct** — Increases threat in Bear forms and reduces the chance enemies detect you while prowling.
- **Brutal Impact** — Increases the stun duration of Bash and Pounce.
- **Thick Hide** — Increases armor gained from items.
- **Feline Swiftness** — Increases movement speed in Cat Form and dodge chance in Cat Form.
- **Feral Charge** — Charges an enemy in Bear Form and interrupts spellcasting.
- **Sharpened Claws** — Increases critical strike chance while in Bear or Cat Form.
- **Improved Shred** — Reduces the Energy cost of Shred.
- **Predatory Strikes** — Increases melee attack power in Cat, Bear, and Dire Bear Form.
- **Blood Frenzy** — Gives combo-point-generating finishing moves a chance to restore Energy.
- **Primal Fury** — Gives Bear crits extra Rage and Cat crits a chance to add an extra combo point.
- **Savage Fury** — Increases the damage of Claw, Rake, Maul, and Swipe.
- **Faerie Fire (Feral)** — Lets you cast Faerie Fire in Cat and Bear forms.
- **Heart of the Wild** — Increases Intellect, Stamina, and Attack Power bonuses depending on form.
- **Leader of the Pack** — Increases melee critical strike chance for nearby party members.

### Restoration
- **Improved Mark of the Wild** — Increases the effects of Mark/Gift of the Wild.
- **Furor** — Gives a chance to gain Rage or Energy when shifting into Bear or Cat Form.
- **Improved Healing Touch** — Reduces the cast time of Healing Touch.
- **Nature's Focus** — Gives healing spells a chance to avoid interruption from damage while casting.
- **Improved Enrage** — Instantly grants Rage when using Enrage.
- **Reflection** — Allows part of your mana regeneration to continue while casting.
- **Insect Swarm** — Damages the target over time and reduces its chance to hit.
- **Subtlety** — Reduces threat caused by healing spells and reduces the chance your helpful spells are dispelled.
- **Tranquil Spirit** — Reduces the mana cost of Healing Touch and Tranquility.
- **Improved Rejuvenation** — Increases the effect of Rejuvenation.
- **Nature's Swiftness** — Makes your next Nature spell with a cast time under 10 seconds instant.
- **Gift of Nature** — Increases all healing done.
- **Improved Tranquility** — Reduces threat caused by Tranquility.
- **Swiftmend** — Consumes Rejuvenation or Regrowth on the target to instantly heal.

- **Ranged Weapon Specialization** — Increases damage with ranged weapons.
- **Trueshot Aura** — Grants nearby party members bonus attack power.

### Survival
- **Monster Slaying** — Increases damage done to Beasts, Giants, and Dragonkin.
- **Humanoid Slaying** — Increases damage done to Humanoids.
- **Deflection** — Increases parry chance.
- **Entrapment** — Gives your traps a chance to root enemies.
- **Savage Strikes** — Increases critical strike chance of Raptor Strike and Mongoose Bite.
- **Improved Wing Clip** — Gives Wing Clip a chance to immobilize the target.
- **Clever Traps** — Increases the duration and effect of your traps.
- **Survivalist** — Increases maximum health.
- **Deterrence** — Temporarily increases dodge and parry chance.
- **Trap Mastery** — Makes traps harder to resist and improves some trap effects.
- **Surefooted** — Increases hit chance and resistance to movement-impairing effects.
- **Improved Feign Death** — Reduces the chance your Feign Death is resisted.
- **Killer Instinct** — Increases critical strike chance with all attacks.
- **Counterattack** — An instant counterattack after parrying that roots the target.
- **Lightning Reflexes** — Increases Agility.
- **Wyvern Sting** — Stings the target, putting it to sleep and then applying a damage-over-time effect when it wakes.

### Frost

- **Improved Frostbolt** — Reduces Frostbolt cast time.
- **Elemental Precision** — Reduces the chance enemies resist your Frost and Fire spells.

- **Improved Frost Nova** — Reduces Frost Nova cooldown.

- **Arctic Reach** — Increases the range of Frostbolt and Blizzard and the radius of Frost Nova and Cone of Cold.
- **Frost Channeling** — Reduces mana cost of Frost spells and reduces threat caused by them.
- **Shatter** — Greatly increases critical strike chance against frozen targets.
- **Ice Block** — Encases you in ice, making you immune but unable to act for a short time.
- **Improved Cone of Cold** — Increases Cone of Cold damage.
- **Winter's Chill** — Frost damage spells can apply a debuff that increases Frost spell critical strike chance against the target.
